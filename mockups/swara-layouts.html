Swara layout + palette mockup

Four layouts for the same raga, in the palette you pick. Move over a layout to see the finger trail and hover, and click to "play" a swara. Unreachable swaras then grey out by arohana/avarohana, same rule as the app.

Notation: dot below = mandra (Ṣ), no dot = madhya, dot above = tara (Ṡ).
